---

State payments for subsoil use licences are calculated in monthly calculation indices (MCI, МРП). From 1 January 2026 one MCI equals **KZT 4,325** (Article 7 of the Law on the Republican Budget for 2026–2028). The amounts below use this value; in another year they must be recalculated with the new MCI. The rules were checked against the texts of the Subsoil Code and the Tax Code as of 11 November 2026.

The cost of a licence is not a single payment. There is a one-off signature bonus, annual payments and obligations, security for liquidating the consequences of the work, and the cost of preparing documents and doing the work itself.

## In short: what each licence costs

| Payment or obligation        | Artisanal mining licence                                                                  | Solid minerals exploration licence                                                       |
| ---------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Signature bonus, one-off     | 9, 12 or 15 MCI depending on area: KZT 38,925, 51,900 or 64,875                           | 100 MCI: KZT 432,500                                                                     |
| Annual subsoil area fee      | Article 621 of the Tax Code sets no rate for artisanal mining                             | From 15 MCI per block per year: KZT 64,875 per block with fewer than 70 blocks           |
| Annual minimum spend on work | Article 192 on minimum expenditure applies only to exploration                            | From 1,200 MCI a year: KZT 5,190,000 for one block in the first three years              |
| Liquidation security         | Bank deposit pledge or bank guarantee; the rate per hectare is set by the regional akimat | Calculated from the number of blocks and the MCI under the authorised body's methodology |
| Licence term                 | 3 years plus one 3-year extension                                                         | 6 years plus one extension of up to 5 years                                              |

Auction licences are not covered here: their signature bonus is set by the auction.

## Signature bonus

The signature bonus is paid once after the licence is issued, no later than 10 working days from issue. It is calculated using the MCI on the date of payment (Articles 762 and 764 of the Tax Code).

| Licence                               | Rate    | At MCI KZT 4,325 |
| ------------------------------------- | ------- | ---------------- |
| Artisanal, area up to 17,000 m²       | 9 MCI   | KZT 38,925       |
| Artisanal, 17,000 to 33,000 m²        | 12 MCI  | KZT 51,900       |
| Artisanal, 33,000 to 50,000 m²        | 15 MCI  | KZT 64,875       |
| Solid minerals exploration            | 100 MCI | KZT 432,500      |
| Solid minerals mining, for comparison | 200 MCI | KZT 865,000      |

An artisanal mining area may be from 500 m² to 5 hectares (Article 269 of the Subsoil Code), so the area-based rates end at 50,000 m².

## Artisanal mining: what else to budget for

- **Liquidation security.** It is provided as a pledge of a bank deposit or a guarantee from a second-tier bank. The amount depends on the area in hectares and the MCI for the year of application, and the rate per hectare is set by the regional executive body (Article 274). The exact amount for a given area is therefore confirmed with the akimat.
- **Reclamation.** After the licence ends, disturbed land must be reclaimed within six months (Article 273-1).
- **Artisanal mining plan and environmental review.** If machinery is needed in the first year, an artisanal mining plan is attached to the application. Work with machinery is allowed only after a positive conclusion of the state environmental review of the plan (Articles 265 and 273).
- **Own equipment.** A truck, drilling equipment, an excavator or a bulldozer must be owned by the licence holder, within the limits of Article 270.
- **Documents.** Copies attached to the application are notarised, and the application and documents are prepared in Kazakh and Russian. The area must be described with corner-point coordinates and its size (Article 265).
- **Consents.** On private land, land of another user or a subsoil area of another person, artisanal mining is possible only with their consent (Article 264).

The application procedure is covered in the guide [on the artisanal mining licence](/en/insights/artisanal-mining-licence-kazakhstan).

## Exploration: annual payments and obligations

**Subsoil area fee.** It is paid for each block every year, no later than 25 February, using the MCI on the first day of the tax period (Articles 621 and 623 of the Tax Code). If the licence was obtained after 1 February, the fee covers the actual months. In the first 36 months the rate is 15 MCI per block with fewer than 70 blocks, 30 MCI with 70 to 120 blocks and 40 MCI with 120 blocks or more. Later the rates rise; the full table is in the guide [on the solid minerals exploration licence](/en/insights/solid-minerals-exploration-licence-kazakhstan).

**Minimum exploration expenditure.** This is not a payment to the state but a mandatory minimum to be spent on work on the area (Article 192 of the Subsoil Code). The MCI as of 1 January of the reporting year is used.

| Exploration years 1–3 | Rate per year | At MCI KZT 4,325 |
| --------------------- | ------------- | ---------------- |
| 1 block               | 1,200 MCI     | KZT 5,190,000    |
| 2–5 blocks            | 1,800 MCI     | KZT 7,785,000    |
| 6–10 blocks           | 2,300 MCI     | KZT 9,947,500    |
| Each block above ten  | plus 120 MCI  | plus KZT 519,000 |

From the fourth year the minimum rises for two or more blocks, and for one block from the seventh year. Geological, geochemical and geophysical work, drilling, trenches, field camps, transport, project documents and other work on the area count towards it. Administrative costs may not exceed 20% of the declared total. Buying a subsoil use right, boundary markers and land surveying, and compensation to land users do not count. If the minimum is not met, the shortfall must be spent and reported within four months after the reporting year. Unremedied breaches on the area fee and minimum expenditure prevent the licence from being extended (Article 193).

**The money for the minimum is needed when you apply.** The application must include proof that the applicant has funds for the first year's minimum expenditure: a Kazakhstan bank statement, a loan or financing agreement, or audited financial statements (Article 187(5)). A geologist or geophysicist on staff, under contract or with a contractor is also required (Article 187(6)).

**Liquidation security.** After reviewing the application, the competent authority sends a notice, and the licence is issued after the security is provided (Article 188). The amount is calculated from the number of blocks and the MCI under the authorised body's methodology (Article 198). The security may be a guarantee, a pledge of a bank deposit or insurance (Article 55(4)).

**Historical costs.** For an exploration or mining licence for solid minerals issued after 31 December 2017 over a territory that was not under subsoil use contracts before 1 January 2018, the payment to reimburse historical costs does not arise (Article 767(2) of the Tax Code). In other cases this must be checked.

## Example: one block, first year of exploration

Suppose an exploration licence for one block was issued in January 2026.

| Item                                | Amount          | Where it goes                  |
| ----------------------------------- | --------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Signature bonus, 100 MCI            | KZT 432,500     | State budget                   |
| Area fee, 15 MCI per block per year | KZT 64,875      | State budget                   |
| Minimum expenditure, 1,200 MCI      | KZT 5,190,000   | Work on the area               |
| Liquidation security                | per methodology | Guarantee, pledge or insurance |

The total is about KZT 5.7 million without the security and without spending above the minimum. This is the legal floor, not a project budget: real work on an area usually needs more.

## Costs the law does not set

Their price depends on the market and the area itself, so we do not give exact amounts:

- preparing the exploration plan or the artisanal mining plan and, where needed, environmental documents;
- coordinates, topography and boundary work;
- geological support: selecting the area, analysing archive materials, fieldwork, sampling and assays;
- a bank guarantee or deposit pledge for the security;
- notarising copies and translations into Kazakh and Russian.

Taxes on extraction and general taxes are a separate topic and are not covered in this guide.
