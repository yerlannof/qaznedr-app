If you need a “gold map of East Kazakhstan” to assess an area, start by identifying the exact location and the year of the map. Older materials labelled “East Kazakhstan Region” may cover land now in Abai Region, established in 2022. Then compare the geological map with reports on work carried out in the [Unified Subsoil Use Platform's report register](https://minerals.e-qazyna.kz/ru/guest/reestr/geological-reports). Check licences and boundaries separately on the [platform's map](https://minerals-map.e-qazyna.kz/ru/start) as of the date of your decision. Neither a point on a map nor an entry in a report proves gold reserves or the right to work in an area by itself.

## First, establish which “East Kazakhstan” the source means

The “East Kazakhstan Region” label on a map does not always match today's administrative boundaries. [Abai Region was established on 8 June 2022](https://www.gov.kz/memleket/entities/mod/press/events/details/15850) from part of the former East Kazakhstan Region. It included Semey and Kurchatov, as well as the Abai, Ayagoz, Aksuat, Beskaragay, Borodulikha, Zharma, Kokpekti and Urzhar districts. **This is the historical list at the time of the division, not a list of today's districts.** Searching only under the current region name may therefore miss a historical report, while an older regional statistic may cover more land than today's East Kazakhstan Region.

For a working query, record the settlement or coordinates of the area of interest, its district on a current map and any known older names. If you have no coordinates, give at least the nearest settlement and an approximate search radius. This information helps you compare materials; it does not imply that the report register itself supports coordinate search. For a historical map sheet, note its year, scale and coverage. Do not apply its regional label to a modern map without checking the location of the specific feature.

## Find the geological base map, then the gold data

A geological map shows rocks, contacts and structures. It helps identify where to seek further information, but it is neither a licence map nor proof of a deposit. A regional sheet helps select an area; more detailed material helps with a local question. The [Geology Committee describes the purpose of 1:50,000 mapping](https://www.gov.kz/memleket/entities/geology/press/news/details/1143562?lang=ru). Enlarging an old scan on screen does not add field observations. Our [general guide to geological maps](/en/insights/geological-map-kazakhstan) explains map types and scales.

If a map marks a gold deposit, mineral occurrence, geochemical anomaly or prospective area, record the **exact symbol class from the legend**. These represent different kinds of information. For a metal-grade record, look for the unit, number and type of samples, range and calculation method: the highest individual sample does not describe the average grade of an orebody. If resources or reserves are given, record the date and reporting standard—GKZ, KAZRC or another named standard. Forecast categories P1–P3 do not become reserves. Review [the differences between classification systems](/en/insights/reserve-classification-gkz-kazrc-jorc-gbt17766) before comparing figures.

## How to search for a report on the area in the platform

Open the [government register of geological reports](https://minerals.e-qazyna.kz/ru/guest/reestr/geological-reports). The page checked has fields for “RGF/TGF”, “Title”, “Author”, “Year” and “Status” (shown in Russian in the service). Try a geographic name in “Title”: the district, nearest settlement or ore field. Repeat the search with the former administrative name and spelling variants; try “золото” (gold) if useful. If you know the author or year, use those fields. Search for a map-sheet number in a report title only if you already know it. The interface does not guarantee that the relevant words appear in the title; no result for one search string does not mean no work was done.

For every relevant result, save the title, author, year, archive number and geographic coverage. Read the abstract or first volume and the list of graphic appendices if available. The [Ministry says](https://www.gov.kz/memleket/entities/mps/press/news/details/945522?lang=ru) the platform includes a report register and first books with abstracts, results and conclusions. The [Geology Committee separately clarifies](https://www.gov.kz/memleket/entities/geology/press/news/details/1160134?lang=ru) that first volumes of secondary reports are available online. An abstract alone does not mean you have examined all maps, samples and primary documentation. Confirm the available set in the official service; QAZNEDR does not offer government archive reports for sale.

Build a simple table for the area: **source — year of work — scale/coverage — data type — what is supported — what remains unknown**. For example, an old report may show that exploration and sampling took place in a zone without providing verifiable digital locations for every point. In that case, establish the location and quality of the original data before discussing conclusions. The [geological materials due diligence checklist](/en/insights/geological-due-diligence-kazakhstan) can help assemble the set.

## Check subsoil use rights separately

Even a convincing geological hypothesis says nothing about whether the area is available. Use the [Unified Subsoil Use Platform map](https://minerals-map.e-qazyna.kz/ru/start) to examine the available information on rights and territory. According to the [Ministry's description](https://www.gov.kz/memleket/entities/mps/press/news/details/945522?lang=ru), the platform provides information on licences and contracts, occupied and unoccupied territories and work carried out. For a specific decision, record the check date, identifiers and status of the entries found. For a significant transaction, have the responsible specialists verify the primary documents and restrictions again. An undated screenshot or third-party map cannot establish current status.

This process does not establish that any particular area is “free” and does not replace a check of the applicable subsoil use procedure. A licence, transfer of rights or transaction assessment is a separate question from the geological map.

## What to send a geologist for a useful answer

Prepare the area name and coordinates or a sketch, the metal of interest, the purpose of the review, the maps and reports found with their years and scales, and a specific question: “Is there enough information to select an area for further checks?” or “What is missing before planning a field visit?” State separately whether you need a review of archive materials, comparison of sources, a field-check programme or a check of the area's legal status.
