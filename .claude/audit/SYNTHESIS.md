# QAZNEDR.KZ — Deep Audit SYNTHESIS (Execution Bible)

Aggregated from 10 dimension audits (design, cleanup, mobile, i18n, SEO, a11y, perf, security, code-quality, conversion). De-duplicated, re-ranked, wave-planned. Verified corrections from the synthesis pass override individual artifact claims and are baked in below.

---

## A. Executive Summary

QAZNEDR.KZ is a structurally-sound Kazakhstan mining-leads marketplace whose **premium promise collapses on the second click**. The homepage ships a genuinely premium editorial system (Cormorant serif, gold-on-ink, atmospheric layers, gold CTAs) that lives in exactly two components and **propagates to zero internal pages** — every catalog, teaser, detail, nav, footer and 404 reverts to generic gray/blue Tailwind. Worse, the product's core funnel is **physically broken**: every listing card links to a locale-less detail route that reads an empty Prisma/SQLite store while the real data lives in Supabase, so every click on a real listing lands on "Объявление не найдено." The same dual-ORM drift poisons SEO (sitemap publishes soft-404 detail URLs), GDPR/audit-logging (writing to the wrong store), and analytics/health (reporting zeros/false-green).

Four themes dominate: **(1) design fragmentation** — the editorial system never escapes the homepage; **(2) dual-ORM breakage** — Prisma/SQLite vs Supabase drift is the root of the broken detail funnel, broken SEO, and legally-wrong GDPR; **(3) i18n leakage** — the entire `/leads` and `/listings` flows are hardcoded Russian (with forbidden emoji headings), so en/zh/kz users see Russian; **(4) conversion friction** — the funnel never answers "is this legal?" / "what do I get for 1M ₸?", has no social proof, and three footer trust links 404.

There are also two confirmed runtime defects in the highest-traffic flows: a React #418 hydration error on `/ru/listings` (bare `area.toLocaleString()` mismatching server/client) and an invalid `<button>`-inside-`<a>` in every listing card.

**Single highest-leverage first move:** fix the listing detail route (Prisma→Supabase + shared transform) AND locale-prefix the card links together — this one coordinated change resurrects the entire listings funnel, un-poisons the sitemap, and unblocks real `generateMetadata`. It is the keystone of Wave 1.
