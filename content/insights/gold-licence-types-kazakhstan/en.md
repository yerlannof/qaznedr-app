---
title: 'Gold licences in Kazakhstan: exploration, mining or artisanal mining?'
description: How a gold exploration licence differs from a solid minerals mining licence and from artisanal placer mining. What to check before choosing an area and applying.
---

A search for a “gold mining licence” can describe quite different goals. Some people first want to find and evaluate a deposit. Others already have reserve data and plan industrial mining. Others want to work a small placer as artisanal miners. The law provides a different right for each case. Gold shown on a map does not, by itself, give anyone the right to start work.

| Your goal                                                                                        | Which regime to check              | Main difference                                                                                                        |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Find a deposit and estimate resources and reserves                                               | Solid minerals exploration licence | Permits exploration, but does not by itself permit industrial mining                                                   |
| Develop a deposit that has already been studied                                                  | Solid minerals mining licence      | Grants the right to mine within the stated area; the application needs resource and reserve data and project documents |
| Recover precious metals by hand or with low-power equipment from a placer or technogenic deposit | Artisanal mining licence           | A special, restricted regime available only to citizens of Kazakhstan                                                  |

## If the deposit still needs to be studied

Start with the [solid minerals exploration licence](/en/insights/solid-minerals-exploration-licence-kazakhstan). It is designed for finding a deposit and estimating its resources and reserves. If a deposit is discovered whose resources and reserves are confirmed by a report, the holder of a valid exploration licence has the exclusive right to obtain a mining licence within the exploration area. This right is exercised **by filing an application with priority**; the licence is granted under the requirements of the Subsoil Code, not automatically. Until a mining licence is obtained, an exploration licence alone does not allow a move to industrial mining.

## If you plan industrial mining

Check the conditions of the [solid minerals mining licence on the EPN](https://minerals.e-qazyna.kz/ru/minerals/gosuslugi/ЛицензияНаДобычу). The government service requires an application, information on the area, a draft mining plan, a draft liquidation plan and a resource and reserve estimation report, or the positive conclusion of the state subsoil examination provided for by the Code. The document set depends, among other things, on whether the exploration licence holder is applying under the exclusive right. A state authority decides whether to grant the licence or to issue a reasoned refusal. The licence does not remove the other conditions for starting work: design and environmental requirements are checked separately.

## If you mean artisanal placer mining

This is a [separate licence with separate rules](/en/insights/artisanal-mining-licence-kazakhstan). It covers artisanal recovery of precious metals and stones from placer and technogenic deposits by hand or with low-power equipment. Only a citizen of Kazakhstan may obtain it, one person may hold only one such licence, and the right under it cannot be transferred. Not every place with gold is suitable for artisanal mining: the areas and restrictions must be checked before applying. The artisanal regime is not a substitute for an industrial licence.

## Where to start

1. Define your goal: **searching and evaluating**, **mining a studied deposit** or **artisanal mining**.
2. Clarify the deposit type and the work method. The conditions differ for a small placer and for a hard-rock deposit; project size alone does not determine the licence.
3. Check boundaries and existing rights on the [EPN](https://minerals.e-qazyna.kz/ru/contracts-map), then open the page of the relevant [government service](https://minerals.e-qazyna.kz/ru/minerals/gosuslugi). A geological map shows subsoil data, but does not confirm that an area is available for licensing.
4. If you are offered a right that has already been granted, check the licence type and its transfer rules separately: see the [guide on transferring subsoil use rights](/en/insights/subsoil-rights-transfer-permission-kazakhstan). An artisanal mining licence cannot be transferred.

You can study published descriptions on the [areas page](/en/leads). The status of each area must be checked as of the date shown; publishing a description does not mean that QAZNEDR holds the right to the area. The team can help review geological materials and work out which questions to ask before applying. [Describe your task](/en/contact?service=geology): region, type of gold, the materials you have and the purpose of the work.

This material is for reference only. Check the right to a specific area, the documents, restrictions and the current wording of the rules before applying.
